Questions

The things people ask first

Does my data really stay on my computer? +

Yes. The tools are ordinary web pages that do their work in the browser, so your figures are only held in the tab you have open. Files you drag in are read locally. Nothing is uploaded — which is also why there's no "sync across devices": that would defeat the point.

Do I need an account? +

A free account keeps track of which tools you've paid for. The tools themselves don't need to know who you are, and they don't store your numbers.

What happens to my work if I close the tab? +

It's gone unless you exported it. That's deliberate — but each tool warns you before you leave, and you can save a working file to reopen later if you'd rather.

Can I use it on my phone? +

Yes. Every tool is built to work on a phone screen, and a few — the estimator, the open house log, the mileage tools — are designed for it first.

Why is some pricing monthly and some yearly? +

The everyday tools are monthly because they're used every week. The trade packs are seasonal, so they're billed once a year to match how the work actually arrives.

Can I switch between a single tool and All-Access? +

Any time, in either direction. You're billed the difference, not a new full price.
